    About us

    FAQ

    Questions? Answers.

    Can't find what you're looking for? Call +880 1846-379461 or message us on WhatsApp.

    I'm a complete beginner. Can I join?

    Absolutely. Our coaches show you how to use every machine safely and build a plan that matches your current fitness level.

    Do you offer a free trial?

    Yes — your first session is free. and pick a time that suits you.

    Do you have online classes?

    Yes. We run both in-person classes at the gym and live online classes you can follow from home.

    How do I pay for a membership?

    Choose a plan and click "Buy now". You can pay with bKash, Nagad, card or in cash at the front desk — our team confirms every order personally.

    What should I bring?

    Comfortable workout clothes, clean indoor shoes, a towel and a water bottle. Lockers are available.

    What are your opening hours?

    Please call +880 1846-379461 for today's timings. Class times are listed in the schedule above.
